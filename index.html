Looking Ahead

A stronger CTC. A stronger Tech.

The Club Team Council is one of the most important bodies in student life at Brooklyn Tech. When it works well, clubs flourish, students connect, and this school becomes something truly special.

My vision for CTC is one where the VP isn't just a title-holder — but a genuine advocate, a problem-solver, and a bridge between students and administration. I want to build lasting systems: better communication, smoother processes, and a culture of real accountability.

Together, we can make the CTC something every club actually wants to engage with.
